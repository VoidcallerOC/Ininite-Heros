Our origin story

Built for the love of comics.

Infinite Heroes Comics is an independent local shop created by someone who has spent a career in the world of comic books.

Meet the owner

Paul Santos has been in the story for decades.

Owner Paul Santos has worked in the comic book industry for more than 20 years. What began with an internship at Warner Bros. became a career as an editor at DC Comics.

After returning to Connecticut from California, Paul opened Infinite Heroes Comics to pursue a long-held dream: owning a local comic shop and sharing the things that make the medium special.
