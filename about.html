02 — Meet the owner

Paul Santos has been in the story for decades.

Paul started with an internship at Warner Bros. and spent years as an editor at DC Comics. After California, he came back to Connecticut and opened Infinite Heroes so Watertown would have a real comic shop on Main Street.

The floor is comics-first. Cards and collectibles sit next to the wall. Ask him what to read next.
